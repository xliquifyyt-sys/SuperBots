  • Win 2 rounds to take the match. Each round lasts 90 seconds; if time runs out, the bot with more health wins.
  • Abilities cost energy. Energy regenerates over time; grab energy cells for a boost.
  • Repair kits restore health. The AI wants them too.
  • Use cover: projectiles are blocked by walls and pillars. The AI loses track of you without line of sight.
