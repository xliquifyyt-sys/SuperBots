HOW TO PLAY

Everyone acts at once. Each turn every bot picks one action, then all actions resolve together and you watch the playback.

  • Aim: drag anywhere on the arena. The dotted line previews your trajectory. Its length depends on your bot's accuracy.
  • Actions: Jump and Missile are always available. Your two Specials have multi-turn cooldowns.
  • Fire: tap FIRE to lock your action. When the timer runs out, your current aim is used (or you skip if you never aimed).
  • Camera: mouse wheel or pinch to zoom, two-finger drag or right-drag to pan, ⌖ to recenter.
  • Elimination: reach 0 HP, fall into the kill floor, or leave the map. Bots self-destruct on death and damage nearby enemies.
  • Power-ups appear on the map. Touch one to grab it instantly, even mid-jump; buffs apply the same turn and last 4 turns. Tap a bot to see what it is holding.
  • Hazards are announced one turn ahead: air strikes, crushers, geysers, rising lava, gusts, reactor pulses.
  • Sudden Death: if everyone left dies in the same turn, they respawn with 60 HP, no specials, and must alternate Jump and Missile.
  • Win: last bot (or team) standing. Optional turn cap: highest HP wins.
