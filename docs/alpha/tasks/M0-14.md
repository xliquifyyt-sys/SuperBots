# M0-14: AI discounts power-ups that are about to expire

Found in M0-04 review: power-ups now expire after `TURN.powerupExpire` turns (each carries
`turnsLeft`), but `src/ai/planner.js` (~line 272) scores a power-up the same whether it has 6
turns left or 1. A power-up on its last turn can still be taken during that turn's play, so this
is a small scoring refinement, not a correctness bug: the AI should not plan multi-turn approaches
toward a power-up that will vanish before it arrives.
