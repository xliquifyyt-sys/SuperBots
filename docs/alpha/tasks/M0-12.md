# M0-12: Online host-settings grid overflows and its targets are under 48px

Found in M0-07 (screenshots from `SHOT_DIR=... node test/online-maps.mjs` at 390x844, 844x390
and 1280x720). The `#online-settings` layout in `style.css` has these problems:

- **390x844:** the 11-option Map row (`.seg`, one flex row) is wider than the screen. "Cloud
  Steps" onwards is clipped, so those maps can't be picked on a phone. The Mode, On turn cap,
  Air strikes, Map hazards and AI difficulty rows are clipped on the right as well.
- **1280x720:** the second `.settings-grid` column (Plan timer, On turn cap, Air strikes, AI
  difficulty) runs off the right edge of the viewport.
- **All sizes:** `.seg button` is `min-height: 36px` and measures 46px tall. The bar is 48px.
- `.setting label` is 11px, below the 12px minimum.

## Fix (suggested)

Let the Map row wrap or become a scrollable chip list or picker, as long as every option is
reachable. Keep `.settings-grid` within its panel at every size. Set `.seg button` to at least
48px and the labels to at least 12px.
