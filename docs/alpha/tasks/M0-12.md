# M0-12: bot ends a turn sunk 0.35 into the Nimbus pillar after a rescue-vent teleport

Found during M0-05 (QA). Severity **P1** (wrong physics result: a bot rests inside solid
terrain; it is the last hit that keeps `node test/bughunt.mjs 5` red once M0-05's step-rect
false positive is removed). Not a crash or desync: the sim is deterministic, so every
client sees the same wrong position.

## Repro

- `node test/bughunt.mjs 5` (or `node test/terrain-embed.mjs 5` for a hull-accurate report).
- Run index 4 of bughunt: mode `ffa`, AI `normal`, 8 players, powerups `high`, airStrikes
  `normal`, requested map `frozenkeel` (cannot seat 8) -> `Match` picks `nimbus`, seed **9402**.
- Turn 9: Volt is hit by a siege shell (55, crit) at 15.67,18.43, flies off the bottom island,
  touches the left rescue vent (pad at 5,20, `to: [25, 7.5]`) and is teleported to 25,7.5.
  Gravitas is already standing on the pillar at that exit. The bot-bot push drives Volt down,
  the resolve ends with Volt `grounded`, at rest, at **24.986, 7.775**: its hull bottom
  (+0.57) is at 8.35, i.e. **0.35 inside** the pillar `R(22, 8, 6, 1.4)`. Gravitas rests on
  top of Volt at 25.02,6.44.
- Step trace (scratch script, mirrors bughunt settings): at t=1.667 the teleport event fires
  (`from [6.69,19.66] to [25,7.5]`), after that step Volt is at 24.991,8.113 with v=0; the
  next step ends the resolve with Volt at 7.775.

## Likely causes (gameplay to decide)

1. `src/core/sim.js` teleporter pads (~line 903) drop the bot at `pad.to` without checking
   that the exit is free, so two bots can share the exit point.
2. The bot-bot separation (~line 928) runs after terrain resolution in the same step and can
   push a bot into terrain; `done()` then ends the resolve because both bots are at rest.
3. Minor, map data: `nimbus` pad exit `to: [25, 7.5]` is below the resting centre of every
   hull (hull bottoms are 0.57 to 0.62, the pillar top is y=8, so rest y is about 7.38 to 7.43).
   On its own this is corrected by terrain resolution; fixing it alone does not fix the repro.
