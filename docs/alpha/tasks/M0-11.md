# M0-11: Botpedia/lobby card text meets the 12px minimum at phone width

Found in M0-01: `style.css` has `.card .cls` at 11px and `.card .stats` at 10px, below the 12px minimum at 390 wide.

## Acceptance
- [x] No text in Botpedia or lobby cards renders below 12px at 390x844 (assertion appended to test/browser.mjs that reads computed font sizes).
- [x] Screenshots at 390x844 and 1280x720 show the cards still fit without overflow; browser gate green.

## Log
- 2026-10-15 (client-ui): `style.css` card text raised to the 12px floor: `.card .cls` 11->12px,
  `.card .stats` 10->12px (row gap 4px so wrapped chips stay tight), `.card .skill` 11.5->12px,
  `.card .passive` 11->12px; lobby small cards get `.small-cards .card .stats span { padding: 1px 4px }`
  so the 12px chips fit the 118px card. No markup or JS changes.
- Gate: appended a block to `test/browser.mjs` (40 added lines, none edited) that opens Botpedia and the
  lobby at 390x844 and 1280x720, reads the computed font-size of every visible text-bearing element in
  each `.card`, and fails on anything below 12px, any element sticking out of its card, a card whose
  scrollWidth exceeds its clientWidth, or fewer than 8 cards. Against origin/main's style.css the block
  fails (`.cls 11px`, stats `SPAN 10px`, `.skill 11.5px`, `.passive 11px`); with this change it passes
  with min 12px in all four cases.
- Verified: all CLAUDE.md gates green locally (determinism 6, rules, headless 20, features, bughunt 2,
  balance-gate 2, online, online-browser, browser). Separate Playwright pass at 390x844, 844x390 and
  1280x720: min card text 12px, no overflow, document width equals viewport width (no horizontal scroll).
