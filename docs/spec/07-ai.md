# 07. AI planner

`src/ai/planner.js`. One planner serves every AI bot in a match. It is deterministic given the world state and the match seed, and it only uses the public preview function (`world.previewAction`) that the player's aim guide also uses, so it never sees anything a human could not.

## Difficulty tiers

| Setting | Name | aimNoise | samples | specialIQ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| easy | Rookie | 0.12 | 40 | 0.4 |
| normal | Veteran | 0.05 | 90 | 0.7 |
| hard | Elite | 0.025 | 160 | 0.95 |

Aim noise is multiplied by the bot's accuracy class (low 1.55, medium 1.15, high 0.75) and added to the chosen aim vector, so an inaccurate bot misses more. `samples` scales how many aims are tried. `specialIQ` scales special scores and gates probabilistic specials (Deflector, Bastion Wall).

## Planning a turn (`plan(b)`)

1. Compute available actions, enemy list, and `danger` at the bot's current position.
2. Compute `threat`: enemies within 30 units add 10 (if within 14) or 6, multiplied by a dodge factor by weight (light 1.35, medium 0.9, heavy 0.45) and 1.5 when below 45% HP. Threat rewards moving away from where enemy shots will land.
3. **Projectile candidates**: for Missile and any projectile special off cooldown (Siege Shell, Ember Spit, Static Field, Anchor Bolt, Pinball, Split Shot, Singularity, Toxic Bomb), evaluate a grid of aims (angles from 160 to 380 degrees, powers 0.35 to 1), score each via the preview, keep the best, then refine around it with smaller perturbations. Pinball also tries bounce counts.
4. **Instant specials**: Chain Arc (line through the most enemies, bonus when the arc target is near), Shockwave (count of enemies within radius 5.4), Gale Shot (nearest enemy within 7.5; large bonus if the push sends them over the kill floor or off a ledge), Deflector (more likely when hurt, threatened, or in danger), Bastion Wall (places a wall between the most exposed teammate, self included, and that teammate's nearest enemy, using the point-target aim).
5. **Jumps**: for Jump, Molten Slam, Updraft and Blink Strike, evaluate landing spots. Blink Strike samples points flanking each enemy (+/-2.2 and +/-3.2 horizontally, 0.4 above) plus six random ground spots, using the point-target aim. Jump samples upward aims. Score = danger reduction, dodge value from threat, power-up proximity, spacing from enemies, closing distance when no shot exists, special-specific bonuses (slam enemies within radius, blink within 3.2 of an enemy), minus a camping penalty when standing still under fire for 3+ turns.
6. Pick the best-scoring candidate; apply aim noise; return `{type, aim, param}`.

## Danger map (`dangerAt`)

Adds: proximity to the kill floor (within 2.2 units), announced hazard zones, circles and points (danger 45/30/35, warning 25/15/18), exposure during an air strike warning when no terrain is overhead (45 now, 30 next), burning patches 20 (not Magmaw), live mines within 1.9 (28), static and toxic fields (15 / 25), and clumping with 2+ enemies within 2.5 (10 each).

## Projectile scoring (`scoreProjectile`)

Uses the preview impact. Discards out, expire, floor. Per enemy within blast radius + 0.5: damage (+40 if it would kill, +12 Anchor Bolt, +10 Static Field, +8 Ember Spit) plus a centre bonus; near misses give a small gradient for refinement. Self-hit within radius + 0.3 costs 1.5x damage; teammates in the blast cost damage (friendly fire on) or 8. Special cases: Singularity scores clusters (22 per enemy within 7 when 2+, plus a floor-pull bonus), Toxic Bomb scores 26 per enemy within 2.6 minus self-proximity, Split Shot sums all four children, a mine impact scores enemies near the mine.

## Behaviours this produces

- Shoots the standing target when it has a line; leads nothing (simultaneous turns), but dodges when threatened.
- Uses walls when hurt or when a teammate is exposed; Chain Arc through cover; Singularity on clusters; Gale Shot toward ledges; body-slams to deliver contact power-ups.
- Retreats from announced hazards and gets under cover before an air strike; grabs Repair Kits when hurt.
- Heavy bots hold position more; light bots dodge more.

## Headless use

Because the planner and the sim have no DOM dependencies, whole matches run in Node (`test/headless.mjs`, `tools/bughunt.mjs`). This is also how a server would validate or drive AI in multiplayer.
